# engine-dev: 2026-09-25T124442_sf2000_win (engine-v2 played, engine-v3 read)

## What produced the win
Real tactic, not just luck: 16...Qb4?! walked into 17.Ra4, and our engine (MVV-LVA
+ quiescence, no TT) correctly found Rxb4/Rc4/Rxb2 winning queen+knight for a
rook (evals.json plies 32-59, eval swings +113→-24 for black, then steadily to
+527). That sequence needed only ~4-5 ply of clean tactical vision, well within
reach of plain alpha-beta + quiescence at 5 s/move — no TT/LMR/killers needed
here. From ply ~40 on the game was a converted material-up endgame; 0 of our
moves lost ≥100cp all game (matches the eval trace).

## Luck assessment
Most of the margin is Stockfish-2000's own noise, not our brilliance: SF
dropped ≥60cp on 9 of its ~41 moves (34, 54, 132, 137, 66, 69, 72, 126, and a
9018cp game-ending blunder 38...Rxc2?? walking into mate). Once we were up a
rook (ply 40, +186), the rest of the game was routine technique against an
opponent that kept handing back more material. Against a less blundering
(higher-Elo or full-strength) opponent this margin disappears — the win says
little about our search depth being adequate yet.

## Fragility found in engine-v3 source (main.rs/eval.rs)
- `Search::quiesce` (main.rs:119-158): when `in_check`, it searches *all* legal
  evasions (not just captures) but recurses into `quiesce` again, never
  `negamax` — so repetition/50-move/insufficient-material checks (added in the
  repetition-detection round, `is_repetition`/`draw_score`, lines 61-98) are
  **never applied inside quiescence**. A forced perpetual-check line reachable
  only through check evasions will be scored by raw eval at `MAX_PLY` (96)
  instead of recognized as a draw. Didn't bite in this game (no perpetual
  tries), but is a real blind spot for saving/losing drawish endgames.
- `Search::best_move` (main.rs:160-190) never stops iterative deepening once a
  forced mate is proven (no `alpha.abs() >= MATE - MAX_PLY` early break) —
  wastes the full 5 s budget after mate is found. No effect on this game
  (mate was played on the last available move), but worth fixing alongside the
  already-queued mate-score UCI reporting since both touch the same loop.
- Contempt gating in `main()` (main.rs:256) uses one static `evaluate(&pos)`
  call before the search even starts, not a searched score — a position that
  looks fine statically but is tactically lost would still get +30cp contempt
  applied, discouraging a repetition/draw we actually need.

## Ranked suggestions (does not change queue order — TT/PVS+LMR/killers still top priority; these are additive, cheap fixes surfaced by this game)
1. **Fix missing draw detection inside check-evasion quiescence** — `engine/src/main.rs::Search::quiesce`, apply the same repetition/50-move/insufficient-material guard used in `negamax` (lines 92-99) to the `in_check` branch before recursing. Effort: ~30 min. Elo: small but asymmetric-risk fix (+5-15 Elo, mainly avoids losses in perpetual-check endgames against stronger opponents). Test: unit test analogous to `avoids_repetition_when_winning`/`repetition_scores_as_contempt_draw` but forcing the position through a check-evasion sequence inside quiescence; then A/B gauntlet vs current build.
2. **Root aspiration windows** — `engine/src/main.rs::Search::best_move`, narrow `alpha`/`beta` around the previous iteration's score (e.g. ±50cp, widen and re-search on fail-high/low) instead of always searching `(-INF, -alpha)` at the root. Effort: ~1 h; do it in the same session as the queued PVS+LMR work since it touches the same loop/signature. Elo: +15-30 (more depth per 5 s once ordering allows tighter cuts — bigger once TT lands). Test: nodes-to-depth-N comparison + gauntlet vs previous build.
3. **Early mate-distance cutoff in iterative deepening** — `engine/src/main.rs::Search::best_move`, break the depth loop once `alpha.abs() >= MATE - MAX_PLY`. Effort: ~15 min. Elo: ~0 today, but prerequisite hygiene for the already-queued mate-score UCI reporting (avoids re-searching a proven mate at higher depth and printing garbage `score cp` lines in the meantime). Test: unit test that `best_move` returns after fewer depth iterations on a forced-mate FEN; check UCI `info` output stays sane.
4. **Search-based contempt gating instead of static eval** — `engine/src/main.rs::main` (line 256), replace the pre-search `evaluate(&pos) < -300` check with a cheap shallow search (e.g. reuse depth-2 negamax score, or just widen the eval margin) before deciding contempt. Effort: ~20 min. Elo: small, edge-case safety net for not refusing a needed draw when statically-okay-but-tactically-lost. Test: construct a FEN that's static-eval-neutral but tactically lost in 2-3 ply, verify contempt is *not* applied.
