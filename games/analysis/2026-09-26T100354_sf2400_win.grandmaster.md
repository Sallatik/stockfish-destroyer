**Ranked action items:**
1. Before sending a piece deep into enemy territory for a pawn with a limited retreat (Nxb7 here has one square, c5), check the eval immediately after the capture, before the opponent's best reply — if it's already down over a pawn, the "free" pawn isn't free. d4 was calm and fine at the same node budget.
2. In decisively winning positions, prefer the most active/fastest-progress move when several moves all keep the win (29.Kh1 vs Rb1, 35.Qf6+ vs Qf7+, 36.c4# vs Rg5#) — cosmetic here, but matters when the margin is thinner.
