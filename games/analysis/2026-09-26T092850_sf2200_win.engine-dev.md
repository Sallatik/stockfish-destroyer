## Root-cause check: Rad1 vs Bxf5

Ran `bin/destroyer-engine-v4` (matches commit c95290d) on the ply-29 FEN with `go movetime 5000` (game condition) and `movetime 25000`:

```
depth 1  cp 216  nodes 1,121        -> a1d1 (Rad1) already "best" at depth 1
depth 2  cp 216  nodes 5,476
depth 3  cp 223  nodes 27,474
depth 4  cp 208  nodes 310,318
depth 5  cp 199  nodes 1,445,346
depth 6  cp 189  nodes 11,490,932   -> deadline hit; bestmove a1d1
(25s budget still doesn't finish depth 7: ~8-10x branching factor/ply means
 depth 7 needs ~80-100M nodes at ~2.3M nodes/s)
```

Rad1 is picked from ply 1 onward and never re-evaluated once Bxf5's real point
(removing the knight that hits Be3, then exploiting the weakened e6/f5 structure
with Ng5+Qh5) becomes visible — that payoff needs ~7+ ply, one ply past what we
reach in 5s. The MG rook PST bonus for d1 (+17) vs a1 (-19) — a genuine +36cp
static gain — is real and legitimate positional info, not an over-reward; it's
just the only thing visible at the depths we actually search, so it wins by
default while the deeper tactical refutation is invisible. Move ordering is
MVV-LVA + hash-move only; there is no killer/history table and no null-move/LMR,
so quiet moves (including Rad1 itself) are searched in generator order with a
branching factor of ~8-10x/ply — that's the actual mechanism starving the depth,
not the eval. The same Rad1 pattern (-119cp) in another SF2200 win confirms this
is systematic, not a one-off.

## Diagnosis
1. **Move-ordering-driven depth ceiling** (no killer/history for quiet moves) is the dominant cause: ~8-10x nodes per ply means we hit the 5s wall at depth 6 in busy middlegames, one ply short of the tactic here.
2. **No null-move/LMR**: nothing cheaply prunes the many quiet sidelines, compounding (1).
3. Eval/PST is not implicated — it correctly ranks Bxf5 once search reaches it; this is not a tuning fix.

## Ranked items (given this game's evidence — reordering of the existing queue, not new proposals)
1. **Promote killer + history move ordering ahead of check-extensions/king-safety.** In `Search::negamax` (engine/src/main.rs), after hash-move ordering, add two killer slots per ply and a `[from][to]` (or `[piece][to]`) history table incremented on beta cutoffs by quiet moves; sort quiets by history score. This directly targets the observed 8-10x branching factor. Est. **+60-100 Elo**; test: A/B gauntlet `bin/destroyer` vs pre-change build at fixed movetime, plus re-run this exact FEN and confirm bestmove flips to Bxf5 within 5s.
2. **Promote null-move pruning ahead of PVS/LMR/aspiration.** Needs a workaround since `shakmaty::Chess` has no native null-move (no legal "pass"); likely requires constructing a position with side-to-move flipped and en-passant cleared via FEN round-trip, or a dedicated null-move helper. Pairs with (1) to compound depth gains (target depth 6→9-10 in this kind of position). Est. **+50-80 Elo**; test: same gauntlet + verify no illegal-move/zugzwang regressions in king-and-pawn endgames (disable null-move when `pos` has only king+pawns).
3. **LMR only after (1) and (2) land**, reducing late quiet moves 1-2 ply once ordering is trustworthy enough not to prune away the real best move. Est. **+30-50 Elo** on top; test: same fixed-movetime gauntlet, watch for tactical blindness regressions on tagged tactics test set if one exists.

No PST/eval changes recommended from this game.
