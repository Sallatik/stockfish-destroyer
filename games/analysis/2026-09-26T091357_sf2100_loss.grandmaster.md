# Grandmaster review: 2026-09-26T091357_sf2100_loss (Destroyer 0.4.0, White, vs SF 2100, loss by mate, 72 ply)

## Verdict
White got a fine, slightly-better position out of the opening (French Advance, up to +0.9 after 15.dxc5), then self-destructed with three consecutive kingside pawn pushes (h4, gxh5, hxg6) that ripped open the g/h-files in front of its own castled king while Black's king was still safely in transit to the queenside. Once those files were open and Black had rooks/knight/queen aimed at h1, White kept playing queenside pawn grabs (b4-b5xc6, bxc6) as if nothing was happening and walked into a forced mate. This is not a strategic loss, it's a self-inflicted king hunt combined with blindness to the mating attack once it existed.

## Recurring weaknesses
- **Self-inflicted king exposure**: pawn storms in front of one's own castled king (g4/h4/gxh5/hxg6) are played as generic "win material / open lines" moves without checking which king actually benefits from the open files.
- **No king-danger override**: once behind, the engine keeps executing an unrelated plan (queenside pawns, capturing loose material) instead of switching to defense/consolidation when the opponent has visibly massed heavy pieces near the king.
- **Horizon/tactical blindness under attack**: b5 and bxc6 were played eval-mate-in-N moves before the actual mate — the engine's 5s/no-TT search isn't finding the forced mating sequence even one tempo out, so it can't route around it via a quiet defensive move like Nh2/Qd2.

## Ranked concrete action items
1. **Add a king-safety eval term now (own-king pawn shield + open-file/attacker-proximity), ahead of null-move/LMR in the queue.** This game shows the engine both creates king weaknesses (self-inflicted h4/gxh5/hxg6 opening its own files) and ignores existing ones (b5/bxc6 while mated shortly after). Score: pawn shield integrity in front of a castled king, open/semi-open files and diagonals toward own king, and count of enemy major pieces within a few ranks of it.
2. **Move check-extensions/quiescence fixes ahead of killer/history in the queue.** The root tactical failure is that at 5s/no-TT the search doesn't see the forced mate one ply before Nh2/Qd2 were needed — it needs to look deeper into forcing lines (checks, king-side captures) specifically when the king-danger term above is high, not just generically.
3. **Add a "king danger" search bias/override**: once the king-danger score crosses a threshold, prefer candidate moves that reduce attacker material/open lines near the king (e.g., Nh2, Qd2) over materially-tempting but irrelevant captures/pushes elsewhere on the board (b5, bxc6 grabbing a bishop while facing mate).
4. **Specific heuristic: penalize pushing a castled king's own shield pawns (g/h-file) to attack/trade unless it wins material outright or clearly targets the opposing king's actual location.** h4 and gxh5 were played while Black's king hadn't even committed to a side yet — the files opened toward White's own king first.
