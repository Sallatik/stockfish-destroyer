## Diagnosis
- **Horizon, not move ordering.** Replaying the real move list (`position startpos moves ... b1d2 d6f6`) on `bin/destroyer-engine-v4` reproduces the game exactly: depth 6 in ~4.9s, `bestmove h2h3`. (`evals.json`'s `fen_before` for this ply drops the h2 pawn — a bug in the eval pipeline's display string, not the engine; verified/corrected by replaying the real moves before testing.) `best_move`'s root loop (`main.rs:199-230`) searches every legal move to full depth each iteration with no root cutoff, so ordering can't change the depth-6 winner, only how fast depth 6 arrives. `bin/stockfish` at full strength on the corrected position doesn't prefer Nc4+ (`d2c4`) until depth ~9 and keeps revising its score after that — this needs real calculation, not a 1-ply check bonus.
- Discovered checks are invisible to *any* cheap move-ordering heuristic, not just MVV-LVA: the checking piece (queen) never moves, so nothing about the candidate move `Nd2-c4` flags it. shakmaty 0.27 has no `gives_check(&Move)` probe, only `Position::is_check(&self)` on the resulting position — detecting it pre-sort would need a full speculative clone+`play_unchecked` per candidate at every node, unlike check extensions, which reuse the `child` already built for the real recursive call.
