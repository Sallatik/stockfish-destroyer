## King PST check
`eval.rs` `MG_KING` only rewards the king's own square (g1 = +24, h1 = +14) and has zero coupling to pawn-shield integrity or open files — there is no term that fires when the g/h pawns in front of the king are traded off. So h4/gxh5/hxg6 looked "free" (even pawn-up-ish) to the static eval while objectively wrecking White's own king shelter. This is the eval blind spot, not a search bug.

## Diagnosis (ranked by cost)
1. **Missing king safety (pawn shield / open file near own king)** — directly explains 3 of 5 blunders (717cp combined) and is the real reason the game was strategically dead by move 22.
2. **No pruning (null-move/LMR) + no TT in this build** — caused generally shallow depth, which is why the deep saving/losing tactics after move 43 were invisible in both directions, though by then the game was already lost.
3. Weak move ordering (quiets unordered, no killer/history) compounds #2.

1. **King safety terms in `eval.rs::evaluate`** — add: (a) pawn-shield bonus for own pawns on f/g/h (or a/b/c) two/one ranks in front of a castled king, (b) semi-open/open-file penalty for files adjacent to the king, scaled by MG phase only (fade out in EG). ~20-30 lines, no new data structures. Est. **+40-70 Elo**; would have flagged h4/gxh5/hxg6 as bad well before the position collapsed. Test: gauntlet `bin/destroyer` vs current build, plus replay this exact game and confirm h4/gxh5/hxg6 are no longer chosen at the same depth.
