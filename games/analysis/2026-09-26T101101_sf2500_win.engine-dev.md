## Diagnosis: passed-pawn term, not depth
`eval.rs::evaluate` is pure material+PST. `EG_PAWN` rewards a pawn already on rank 6-7 (c6 = +85, c7 = +158) but is flat on rank 4 (c4 = -3), so with the pawn still on c4, "grab a loose pawn/rook" and "advance the passer" score about the same, and the push only wins out once nothing else is left to grab. The true forced win from move 37 is 20-30 plies deep — out of reach even with null-move/PVS/LMR (already queued); a static passed-pawn bonus is what gives the search a compass at practical depths. A mop-up/king-distance term would help the separate move 41-53 king-and-rook-chase stretch, but that stretch wasn't losing eval, just meandering, so it's secondary to this stall.
