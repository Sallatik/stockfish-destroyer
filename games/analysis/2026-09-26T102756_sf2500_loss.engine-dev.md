## Focus 1: the rook shuffle, plies 77-83
Replayed the ply-77 FEN `8/2k2p2/3r4/2p4R/3p2P1/8/P2R1PPK/2r5 w - - 1 39` with `bin/destroyer-engine-v6` at 5s: it reaches **depth 10** (17.9M nodes) and converges cleanly on **Rd3** (matches the game) at **+57cp** in its own eval, while Stockfish's full-strength eval of the resulting position is **-483**. Depth is adequate (10 ply covers the pawn pushes at issue) and the choice is not noisy/flat — it is a *confident, wrong* evaluation: `eval.rs::evaluate` is pure material+PST, so it credits White's extra pawn (4 vs 3) and is blind to Black's connected c5/d4 passers 2-3 squares from queening, the infiltrated Rc1, and White's boxed-in Kh2. Verdict: **eval blind spot, not depth and not move-ordering noise.**

## Focus 2: king passivity (g7/f6 instead of Kg3)
Same root cause, different phase. By ply 107/109 material is already lost (R+P vs 4P), but `evaluate()` has no notion of "king should escort/blockade a passed pawn": `EG_KING`'s PST only rewards generic centralization, not proximity to the actual pawn race, so the search happily spends tempi pushing kingside pawns nobody defends instead of marching the king toward the action. Missing term: king-distance-to-nearest-passed-pawn (own and enemy), not gated to "only when clearly ahead" as `mop-up` is currently scoped in the plan.

## Diagnosis (ranked by cp lost this game)

2. No rook-activity / hanging-pawn-safety term — ply 57, and it also muddies move ordering among the similarly-scored rook moves at 77-83.
3. Shallow depth in busy (non-endgame) middlegame positions, ply 17 — secondary; PVS/LMR is already queued below pawn structure, which this game confirms is the right order.

## Plan-queue impact

## Risks

- Mirror carefully for Black (the existing `sq ^ 56` white-only PST trick is a known footgun for new terms); extend the `eval_is_symmetric_for_side_to_move` unit test to cover passed-pawn/king-tropism/rook terms before trusting any gauntlet result.
- Validate the new eval terms together with the pending (untested, working-tree-only) null-move edit specifically in king+pawn races — both interact in exactly this position family (zugzwang-prone endgames), so A/B and regress them jointly, not only in isolation.
- None of this touches `tt.rs` or UCI I/O or `think_time`, so no direct risk to the 5s/move rule or UCI correctness; still, re-run the mate-score/TT round-trip tests in `main.rs`/`tt.rs` after touching `eval.rs` since a sign error there is the classic way to corrupt search results without a crash.
