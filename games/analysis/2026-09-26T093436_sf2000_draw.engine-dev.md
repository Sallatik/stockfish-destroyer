## Diagnosis
1. **Eval blind spot, king safety** (6/9 mistakes). Our `evaluate()` (material+PST only) misjudges these positions by hundreds of cp. Root loop searches all root moves to full width, so MVV-LVA ordering isn't why captures beat Bg4 — the leaf eval is. Proof: at ply45's FEN, `evaluate()` = **+337cp**, Stockfish's real eval = **-50cp** (387cp gap) — nothing in eval.rs prices the self-opened g-file or Black's active B/N/Q. Replayed ply45 with current `bin/destroyer` (engine-v5, TT, depth 8, 8.9M nodes/5s): bestmove is **Qxa4** — still a capture, not Bg4. TT fixed the worst tactical blunder (avoids losing gxf7+) but not the greedy-capture bias, confirming this is eval-driven, not just horizon.
2. **Shallow depth / no check extensions** (ply 47 directly: Rf1 is refuted by ...Qxf7 two plies later and doesn't even defend the pawn it "should").
3. **Contempt gate uses a static-eval snapshot.** Before White's 41st/42nd/44th moves, `evaluate(&pos)` = **+10 to +22cp** (well above the `-300` gate), so the **CONTEMPT=30 "avoid draw" branch fired**, not the "already lost" branch — the engine thought it was fine while Stockfish had it at -497..-680. It fell into the repeat anyway because no line scored better under its own eval. Side finding (arena, not engine): python-chess's `is_repetition(3)` on the final position is **False** (only twofold occurred); `arena/play.py`'s `board.is_game_over(claim_draw=True)` ends the game as soon as Black's *next* move could force a third repetition, without asking Black (Stockfish, clearly winning) to actually play it. This draw was a freebie both ways — worth knowing, not an engine bug.
