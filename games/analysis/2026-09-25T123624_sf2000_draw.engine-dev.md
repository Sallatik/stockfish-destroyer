## Diagnosis: why the 30-ply king hunt (ply 77–115)
1. **Not a repetition artifact.** No repeated position was reachable during ply 77–115: Stockfish's checks were each *distinct* (queen+knight harassment), netting a pawn or square every few moves. The engine wasn't "settling for a draw" — it genuinely had no non-losing move at its search depth. The eventual draw (ply 121–133, Bf1/Be2 vs Kb8/Kc7 shuffle) happens only after material settles into a real R+B-vs-Q+2P fortress-like position, where repeating is *correct*.
2. **Root cause of the bleed is the missing king-safety eval term**, not a repetition bug: material-only/PST eval has no concept of "my king is a perpetual-check magnet," so at each fixed search depth the engine just picks the least-bad move *within its horizon*, then gets re-forked next move — classic horizon effect compounding over many plies.
3. **Quiescence likely amplifies this**: `quiesce()` in `main.rs` expands *all* legal replies while in check with no ply cap other than `MAX_PLY=96`; on forced check chains this can approach that cap, consuming a large, uneven share of the node budget on exactly the exposed-king lines and starving main-search depth there relative to quieter alternatives — reinforcing (not fixing) the shallow horizon in #2.
4. **Current contempt would not have changed this game.** `draw_score`'s 30cp contempt is gated off (`contempt=0`) whenever root static eval < -300 (`main.rs::main`, `go` handler); by ply 19 we were already worse than -300, so contempt was effectively 0 for nearly the whole game — the engine would already have been willing to take the final repetition, and had no earlier repetition option to refuse. No regression risk here.

## Ranked suggestions (new findings only; already-queued items — check extensions, quiescence pruning, mate scoring, king safety — not re-proposed except for reordering)

1. **Reorder the queue: move "check extensions + quiescence pruning + mate score reporting" to the front**, ahead of killer/history and null-move. This game's evidence: 9 of 14 mistakes and the largest cp losses (up to 426cp) cluster exactly in the check-heavy king-hunt phase, not in quiet middlegame play — search behavior under checks is our biggest observed practical weakness right now. Est. +40–70 Elo (unlocks correct depth in exactly the position type SF2000 exploited). Test: rerun this game's ply-77 FEN as a fixed test position, compare nodes-to-depth-8 and bestmove before/after; gauntlet old vs new build.
2. **Add a one-line node-source counter before touching qsearch**: split `self.nodes` into `main_nodes` vs `qsearch_nodes` (or log `info string qnodes=N` per `go`) in `main.rs::negamax`/`quiesce`. ~15 min, zero behavior change, confirms whether qsearch really dominates node budget in check-chain positions before investing in a check-extension cap — de-risks item 1's design (e.g., whether a depth-6 vs depth-3 cap on in-check quiescence extension is needed).
3. **Contempt gate: derive from search score, not a single static-eval snapshot, and add hysteresis.** `main.rs::main`'s `go` handler computes `contempt` once from raw `evaluate(&pos)` with a hard -300 cutoff; this is noise-prone (PST-only eval can sit right at the boundary and flip every move with no memory) even though it happened to work out in this game. Fix: gate on the previous move's *searched* root score (already available from the prior `go`, or first cheap depth-2 probe) and use a two-sided band (e.g., enter "desperate" below -350, only reset above -150) instead of one threshold. ~30 min in `Search::draw_score`/`main`. Est. +5–10 Elo, mainly robustness. Test: unit test feeding a position sequence that oscillates around -300 static eval, assert contempt doesn't flip every ply; gauntlet for regression.
4. **Scope note for the already-queued king-safety work**: when it's implemented, prioritize a cheap "queen/rook on an open line/diagonal to our king with no blocker" penalty over more elaborate pawn-shield/attacker-count scoring first — that's the exact pattern (queen+knight checks with king in the open after early piece trades) that cost ~1600cp here. No separate Elo estimate (already queued); this only affects implementation order within that item.
