**Recurring weaknesses**
1. Quiet moves are chosen essentially at random (material-only eval, so move-generation order decides).
2. No development: the g1 knight never moved and the rooks never connected.
3. No king safety: never castled, and the pawn shield (f3, g3, h3) was pushed early for no reason.
4. Wrong priorities once winning: kept grabbing material and giving checks instead of consolidating.
5. Pawn structure ignored: pushes that leave holes.
6. Tactical luck: without quiescence search, horizon-effect exchanges will lose material against 1500+.

**Suggestions (ranked)**
1. PeSTO-style tapered piece-square tables: minor pieces −30/−50 on the back rank and +10/+30 in the centre; pawn bonus for e4/d4/e5/d5; king bonus for castled squares g1/c1 and −30/−60 for staying on e1/e2 in the middlegame; king centralization in the endgame. 3. King safety: pawn shield +10/+15cp per shield pawn, −20cp per open or half-open file next to the king, −40cp for losing castling rights without castling. Scale by the opponent's non-pawn material.
4. Development/tempo penalties for the first 10–12 moves: −15cp per undeveloped minor piece, −10cp for an early queen sortie, −5cp per extra flank-pawn move.

**Diagnosis**
1. No quiescence search, so the horizon effect is everywhere: odd/even score swings, and positions with hanging pieces are trusted. Biggest Elo sink against 1500–2200.
2. Material-only `evaluate()`: quiet moves are ordered by move generation, and there is no gradient toward converting or mating (explains the knight shuffle in the smoke test).
3. **Bug:** no repetition or 50-move detection. `parse_position` discards the history and `negamax` ignores `halfmoves()`, so threefold draws happen by accident when ahead.
Secondary: no transposition table and weak move ordering, so the branching factor is huge (depth 7–8 where a healthy engine reaches 12–16).

**Improvement plan** (one per round, A/B 200 games at 0.1s)
1. Quiescence search (≈ +150–250 Elo, 30–45 min). Stand-pat, captures + promotions, evasions when in check (capped), MVV-LVA. Move the `depth == 0` check before movegen in `negamax`. Also MVV-LVA in the main search.
2. PeSTO tapered PSTs (≈ +100–200). Phase from N=1, B=1, R=2, Q=4 (max 24); mirror squares for Black.
3. Repetition, 50-move rule and game history (≈ +10–40 self-play, more vs SF). Zobrist hash stack via `shakmaty::zobrist::Zobrist64`; twofold in search = draw; `halfmoves() >= 100` = draw; optional contempt.
4. Transposition table + hash move (≈ +50–100, 1.5–2h). Allocate once, ply-adjust mate scores, verify hash-move legality.
5. Killers + history heuristic (≈ +30–60).
6. Null-move pruning (≈ +50–100). Not in check, depth ≥ 3, has non-pawn material.
7. PVS + LMR (≈ +50–100).
8. Later: check extension, mop-up endgame term, aspiration windows, `score mate N`, pawn structure, king safety.

**Risks**
- qsearch must share the deadline check. Cap in-check evasions or qsearch ply to avoid explosion in perpetual-check lines.
- TT allocated at startup, not inside `go`; check legality of hash moves; ply-adjust mate scores.
- Repetition history must be rebuilt from the full `position ... moves` list each `go`, and pushes/pops must be balanced.
- `stop`/`go infinite` aren't supported (blocking stdin), fine for the arena.
- At 0.1s the A/B runs at depth 5, which understates the gains from pruning features. Spot-check those with 5s games.
